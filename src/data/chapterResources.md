## Coverage Gaps — Official Syllabus Phrases Not Yet a Dedicated Chapter

These are lines lifted straight from the official JEST syllabus PDF that
don't map cleanly onto one of the 35 chapters above. Nothing below needs a
new app chapter necessarily — but each should be folded into an existing
chapter's study session (noted in brackets) rather than assumed covered.

### Mathematical Methods — left to cover
- **Vector algebra and vector calculus, tensors, curvilinear coordinate
  systems** — no dedicated chapter. *(Fold into: study alongside Ch. 1.1–1.2
  as a prerequisite; use Spiegel's Vector Analysis directly — this is
  usually assumed background rather than separately tested, but JEST names
  it explicitly.)*
- **Linear differential equations** (as their own topic, beyond the
  Sturm–Liouville tie-in already under Special Functions) — *(fold into
  Special Functions chapter's book reading: Boas Ch. 8 — Ordinary
  Differential Equations)*
- **Elementary properties of discrete groups** — no chapter, no book
  mapped anywhere in this blueprint. *(This is basic group theory —
  neither Boas nor Arfken's early chapters cover it in depth; you'll need
  a dedicated supplement, e.g. lecture notes on group theory for physicists,
  or the group-theory chapter in Arfken/Weber if your edition includes one.)*

### Classical Mechanics — left to cover
- **Newton's laws, conservation of energy and momentum, collisions** — no
  dedicated chapter (treated as prerequisite background). *(Fold into: quick
  review before Ch. 2.1 — Goldstein Ch. 1 opens with exactly this.)*
- **Symmetry and conservation laws** (Noether's theorem specifically) —
  only partially covered inside the Lagrangian & Hamiltonian chapter.
  *(Fold into: Ch. 2.1's book reading — Goldstein §2.6–2.7 covers this
  explicitly; make sure it isn't skipped as "just Lagrangian mechanics.")*

### Electromagnetism & Optics — left to cover
- **Energy and momentum of electromagnetic fields** (Poynting vector,
  Poynting's theorem) — not explicit in any chapter's book reading.
  *(Fold into: Ch. 3.3's reading — Griffiths §8.1–8.2, immediately before
  the EM-waves chapter Griffiths Ch. 9 already assigned.)*
- **Reflection, refraction of plane EM waves** — not explicit.
  *(Fold into: Ch. 3.3 — Griffiths §9.3, right after the EM-waves sections
  already assigned.)*
- **EM waves in dispersive and conducting media** — not explicit.
  *(Fold into: Ch. 3.3 — Griffiths §9.4, same chapter.)*

### Quantum Mechanics — left to cover
- **Central potentials, hydrogen atom** — this is a real gap. "1D
  Potentials" (Ch. 5.2) does **not** cover this; central potentials are
  fully 3D. *(Fold into: add Griffiths Ch. 4 §4.1–4.2 — Central Potentials,
  The Hydrogen Atom — to Ch. 5.1's or 5.2's reading list explicitly; on
  video, this is covered in Ramadevi's course as a separate 3D-potentials
  module, distinct from the 1D-potentials one.)*
- **Variational principle** — mentioned only as a Sakurai cross-reference,
  no primary reading assigned. *(Fold into: Ch. 5.4 — add Griffiths §7.1
  before the perturbation-theory sections already assigned.)*

### Thermodynamics & Statistical Physics — left to cover
- **Elements of kinetic theory** — not explicit in any chapter's reading.
  *(Fold into: Ch. 6.1 — Reif Ch. 1, or Kittel & Kroemer's early
  kinetic-theory sections, before the thermodynamic-potentials reading.)*
- **Statistics of paramagnetism** — mentioned in the syllabus line for
  Ch. 7.2 but no specific reading assigned. *(Fold into: Ch. 7.2 — Reif's
  paramagnetism worked example, or Pathria & Beale's corresponding section
  in Ch. 3.)*

### Electronics — left to cover
- **LCR circuits, rectifiers, amplifiers, active filters and oscillators**
  — currently folded generically into "Op-Amp Circuits" (Ch. 8.2) and
  "Semiconductor Devices" (Ch. 8.1), but LCR/filter/oscillator design is
  its own topic block in the syllabus line and deserves explicit reading.
  *(Fold into: Ch. 8.2 — add Sedra & Smith's filters/oscillators chapter,
  typically Ch. 12 or later depending on edition, alongside the OPAMP
  chapter already assigned.)*

### Solid State / Atomic-Nuclear-Particle (Units 9–10)
No gap analysis applies — these two units aren't part of the official JEST
syllabus at all (see the note in the main blueprint file), so there's
nothing from the official document to check them against.
