# Physics OS PRD v1.0

## Vision

Physics OS is a personal study operating system for IIT JAM and JEST preparation.

Its purpose is to eliminate friction between opening the laptop and beginning focused study.

The application is designed around the syllabus rather than books and should remember my study state automatically.

---

## Design Principles

- Every click should reduce a study decision.
- Syllabus-first organization.
- Local-first storage.
- Offline PDFs.
- Online video links.
- Dark theme.
- Minimal UI.
- No duplicate data.
- Maximum three navigation levels.
- Everything important within two clicks.
